Our Vision

Healthcare is becoming spatial

Medicine has always been visual — but most of it still exists in flat, two-dimensional formats. MR images are scanned in slices. Surgical training relies on textbooks or watching from the back of an operating room. Post-op complications often go unnoticed until it's too late.

We believe there's a better way. By bringing spatial computing, 3D reconstruction, and intelligent automation into healthcare workflows, we can help doctors make better decisions before they ever pick up a scalpel — and help patients recover more safely after the procedure is over.

The intersection of XR and healthcare is growing at 34% CAGR, yet most tools in this space are still generic, hand-crafted content — not personalized, case-specific understanding of the patient's own body. That's what we're solving.

What We Do

Tools for better healthcare

We're building a suite of tools that span the entire care continuum — from pre-operative planning to post-operative recovery. Each product shares a core capability: turning medical data into actionable, spatial understanding.

Surgical Training & Simulation

Immersive training tools that prepare surgical professionals for any case — from basic spatial visualization to complex, patient-specific scenarios built directly from real surgical data.

Post-Op Care & Prediction

Tools that predict and flag post-operative complications before they escalate — from delirium risk to surgical site issues — so care teams can intervene proactively, not reactively.

Patient Education

Making complex medical information accessible through 3D visualization. Patients understand their conditions and procedures better when they can see them — not just hear about them.

Our Approach

What makes us different

Most medical training and visualization tools are built the same way — hand-crafted, generic, and expensive. We're taking a different approach that changes both the cost structure and what's possible.

Built from real data

Our simulations are generated from actual surgical video and imaging — not hand-modeled generic libraries. That means every training scenario is case-specific, clinically accurate, and a fraction of the cost to produce.

Scale expert knowledge

The skill and judgment of top surgeons shouldn't be limited to one operating room at a time. We're building tools that make expert-level medical capability accessible far beyond the hospital walls.

Human-in-the-loop quality

AI handles the repetitive, mundane work — image processing, pattern recognition, preliminary analysis. Human experts focus on what matters: making the right call for each patient.
