The Team

Founder

KR

Keshav Rungta

Ex Apple · MS & BS, UC San Diego

Keshav is an ML engineer who spent 4 years building and shipping the first spatial computer, the Apple Vision Pro. His experience in 3D spatial computing and understanding of sensor fusion is at the core of ZoiStudio's vision: making healthcare more visual, more personal, and deeply grounded in real data at the beating heart of cutting edge technology and research.
